Checks whether this phone gives real stereo, then uses the two mics to flag sounds from behind. Everything else counts as "middle".

Tap near the top of the phone, then near the bottom. With real stereo, one bar jumps more than the other each time. If the two bars always move together, the phone is sending mono.

2 · Mount + calibrate

Lay the phone flat on top of the headset with its length pointing forward, so one mic is at the front and one is at the back. Then clap about 1 m away, three times for each button.
